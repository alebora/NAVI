## Intent

A typography-led demo companion with three distinct feature chapters. Motion
introduces each idea; real footage then demonstrates it. Black, white, and a
single blue accent. No fabricated robot footage or fake product screenshots.

The 53-second editorial draft reserves 29 seconds for footage. Typography beats
remain 0.6–2 seconds each; demo slots intentionally stay on screen longer so the
audience can understand the physical interaction.

## Structure

- 0–5s: Hook — the problem.
- 5–9s: Opening.
- 9–14s: 01 / Navigation.
- 14–24s: Navigation demo — schematic, swappable for footage.
- 24–29s: 02 / NFC access.
- 29–36s: NFC demo — schematic, swappable for footage.
- 36–42s: 03 / Walking meetings.
- 42–54s: Walking meeting demo — schematic, swappable for footage.
- 54–58s: Closing.

## Assets and truth

No demo videos are available yet. Each demo section plays a labeled
“how it works” schematic built from simple lines and type — not simulated
footage or product screenshots. Replacement instructions: assets/demo/README.md.

Navigation shows a destination request, escort, and arrival.
NFC demonstrates a prototype badge/access check, not proof that an NFC UID alone
provides strong authentication or physical access control.
Walking-meeting footage should show consent before capture and follow behavior.
Use permission-cleared audio/video only. Conceal badge identifiers.

Elevator/arm interaction is not a separate promise in this cut. Add it only
when a filmed, working demonstration exists.

The current composition is deliberately silent. Add cleared demo audio later;
no music or voiceover is assumed.
